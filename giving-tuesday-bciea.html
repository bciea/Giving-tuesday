Awareness is where it starts. Action is where lives change.

Since 2008, BCIEA has been turning awareness into action.

Breast Cancer Initiative East Africa brings lifesaving breast health education, early detection, patient support and hope to underserved communities in Rwanda and beyond.

Through community outreach, village ambassadors, the IROZA 24/7 breast health chatbot, survivor support and innovative health initiatives, BCIEA is reaching women who might otherwise be left behind. But it can't do it alone.

What your gift makes possible

Community outreach and village ambassadors

When young people understand health systems, they become unstoppable agents of change. Your Giving Tuesday gift trains students and youth leaders to champion breast health awareness in their schools, homes, and communities—creating a ripple effect of prevention that protects mothers, sisters, and future generations.

Meet IROZA:

Life-Saving Answers in the Palm of Her Hand For a woman in a remote community, fear, distance, and stigma shouldn't stand in the way of her health. IROZA is the first AI-powered WhatsApp assistant putting free, private, correct breast health guidance directly into women’s hands, 24/7, in Kinyarwanda, English and French. Your Giving Tuesday gift powers this breakthrough innovation, bringing trusted answers and early detection to women anytime, anywhere.

Earlier detection

When cancer is caught late, options shrink. When it is caught early, hope thrives. This Giving Tuesday, your generosity puts vital knowledge and life-saving outreach directly into women’s hands—empowering them to recognize the signs, get screened, and gain what matters most: more options, more time, and a future.

Support that stays with her

Patient and survivor support that helps women overcome the barriers between a diagnosis and the care they need.
